# Director of Product — Routa Logistics Oy

**Location:** Helsinki (Vallila), hybrid; around six trips a year to customers in Sweden and Poland
**Salary:** €9,000–10,500 per month

Routa Logistics builds transport-management software for mid-sized freight forwarders in the Nordics and Central Europe. Around 160 customers plan routes, book carriers and invoice shipments on our platform. The company has 85 employees and is owned by its founders and a Finnish growth fund.

We are looking for a Director of Product to lead our three product teams and report to the CEO.

**Responsibilities**
- Own the product strategy and roadmap
- Lead three product managers and work closely with the Head of Engineering
- Move the organisation from customer-request roadmaps to outcome-based planning
- Work with sales on pricing and packaging for a new mid-market tier

**Requirements**
- 8+ years in product, 3+ leading product managers
- B2B SaaS experience
- Experience introducing product practices such as discovery and OKRs
- Fluent English; Finnish is an advantage
