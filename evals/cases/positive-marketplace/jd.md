# Head of Product, Trust & Safety — Kauppapaikka Nordic

**Location:** Helsinki or remote within the Nordics; about one trip a month to Stockholm or Oslo
**Salary:** €10,500–12,000 per month base, plus bonus

Kauppapaikka Nordic runs consumer classifieds and second-hand marketplaces in Finland, Sweden and Norway, with 4 million monthly users. English is our working language; our product organisation has people of 23 nationalities.

We are hiring a Head of Product for Trust & Safety. You will lead four cross-functional teams covering identity and verification, moderation tooling, fraud prevention, and ratings and reviews, and report to our Chief Product Officer.

**Responsibilities**
- Own the trust and safety strategy across three countries
- Lead four product managers and work in a trio with the engineering and design leads
- Work with customer service on policy (prohibited items, animal welfare, scams)
- Set measurable outcomes for trust and track them with our analytics team

**Requirements**
- 10+ years in product, including leading product managers
- Product leadership in consumer marketplaces or classifieds
- Experience with moderation, trust or platform-integrity products
- Experience leading teams across more than one country
- Data fluency and experience with OKRs or similar outcome frameworks

In the interview process you will meet the CPO, two of your future peers and the Director of Customer Service.
