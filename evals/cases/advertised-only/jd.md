# Product Director — Kirjokallio Health Oy

**Location:** Helsinki, hybrid, flexible hours
**Salary:** €9,500–11,000 per month + equity

At Kirjokallio we believe the best decisions come from evidence, not hierarchy. We are a consumer wellbeing app helping people build sleep and recovery habits, used in 14 countries.

We are proud of our culture. We are **truly empowered**: product teams own outcomes, not features. Leaders here **coach rather than command**. We **celebrate failure** and **learn fast**. Our CEO says "the best idea wins, whoever has it". Psychological safety is our foundation, and **we never let hierarchy decide**. Sustainable pace is a core value: no heroics, no weekend work, ever.

As Product Director you will have **full ownership** of the product vision and strategy and lead our four product teams. You report to the CEO.

**You will**
- Own the product vision and roadmap with real decision rights
- Lead and grow four product managers and two designers
- Run continuous discovery and experimentation at scale
- Shape our world-class, international, kind and demanding culture

**You bring**
- 10+ years in product, 5+ leading product managers
- Consumer subscription experience
- A love of data and of customers
- English (our working language)
