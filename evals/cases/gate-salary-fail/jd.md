# Head of Product — Lumivaara Oy

**Location:** Helsinki (Kalasatama), hybrid 3 days a week
**Salary:** €6,000–7,200 per month, plus a small annual bonus

Lumivaara runs Finland's fastest-growing peer-to-peer rental marketplace for outdoor and sports equipment. Owners list skis, bikes, kayaks and tents; renters book by the day. We have 180,000 registered users and operate in Finland and Estonia.

We are looking for a Head of Product to lead our three product teams (12 people across product, design and engineering) and report to the CEO.

**You will**
- Own the product strategy and roadmap for both sides of the marketplace
- Build trust and safety features: verification, deposits, reviews, dispute handling
- Introduce outcome-based planning and continuous discovery
- Coach two product managers and a product designer

**You bring**
- 8+ years in product, including leading product managers
- Experience with two-sided marketplaces
- Strong analytical skills and comfort with experimentation
- Fluent English; Finnish is a plus
