# Head of Product & Engineering — Sääksi Analytics Oy

**Location:** Helsinki or remote within Finland
**Salary:** €9,000–10,500 per month + options

Sääksi Analytics is a 22-person B2B startup building computer-vision analytics for retail stores. Our seed round closed this spring.

We are looking for a hands-on Head of Product & Engineering who will both decide what we build and build a significant part of it.

**What hands-on means here**
- You will write production code every week (Python, TypeScript) and review every pull request
- You own the system architecture, our cloud costs and our ML deployment pipeline
- You will personally prototype new features end to end before the team picks them up
- You lead three engineers and one designer; there is no separate product manager

**Requirements**
- 8+ years of software engineering, with recent hands-on coding
- Experience deploying machine-learning models to production
- Product sense: you talk to customers and turn what you learn into a roadmap
- Startup experience
