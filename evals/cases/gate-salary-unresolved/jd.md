# Director of Product, Marketplace — Kotikanta Group

**Location:** Helsinki, hybrid. Occasional travel to Stockholm and Tallinn.

Kotikanta Group operates second-hand marketplaces for furniture and home goods in Finland, Sweden and Estonia, with around 1.1 million monthly visitors. We are owned by a Nordic growth investor and have been profitable for two years.

We are hiring a Director of Product to lead our product organisation of five cross-functional teams and to report to the Chief Executive Officer. You will be a member of the management team.

**Responsibilities**
- Set the product vision and strategy for buyers, sellers and professional resellers
- Lead and develop five product managers and the design lead
- Improve trust: seller verification, fraud prevention and moderation
- Build a product operating model with clear outcomes and discovery practices
- Work with commercial teams on the professional reseller offering

**Requirements**
- 10+ years in product, 5+ years leading product managers
- Track record in consumer marketplaces or classifieds
- Experience leading teams across more than one country
- Data fluency: you set metrics and read experiments yourself
- Excellent English

We offer a competitive salary and benefits package.
