**Salary:** €115,000–135,000 per year base

We are hiring a Head of Product for Marketplace Intelligence: pricing recommendations, fraud detection and seller quality, increasingly built on large language models. You lead three product managers and report to the CPO.

**Requirements**
- 10+ years in product, 4+ leading product managers
- Product leadership in two-sided marketplaces
- **You have owned an LLM evaluation framework in production**: rubrics, scoring and human review gates for model output
- **German at C1 or above**: most of our sellers and half the team work in German
- Experience with trust, fraud or moderation products
- Experience leading teams across more than one country
