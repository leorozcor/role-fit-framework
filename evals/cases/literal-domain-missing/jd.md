# VP Product — Verkkovirta Oy

**Location:** Helsinki (Ruoholahti), hybrid
**Salary:** €10,000–12,500 per month

Verkkovirta provides grid-analytics software to electricity distribution system operators in the Nordics and Germany. Our platform forecasts load, detects faults and plans grid investment for 40 network companies.

We are looking for a VP Product to lead product management (six product managers) and report to the CEO as a member of the executive team.

**Responsibilities**
- Own the product strategy across forecasting, fault detection and investment planning
- Lead and develop the product management team
- Work with key customers' grid planning and operations departments
- Shape our roadmap around regulatory change (EU network codes, flexibility markets)

**Required**
- **7+ years of product leadership in electricity grid, utility or energy-network software.** This is essential: our customers expect a VP who speaks the language of distribution system operators from day one.
- Experience leading product managers
- Experience selling to and working with utility customers
- Fluent English; Finnish, Swedish or German an advantage
