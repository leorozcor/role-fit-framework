# Senior Product Manager, Checkout — Kassamatka Oy

**Location:** Helsinki, hybrid

Kassamatka is a Helsinki-based e-commerce platform for independent fashion brands, with around 300 brands and 90 employees.

We are hiring a Senior Product Manager for our Checkout team (five engineers, one designer). You report to the Head of Product.

**You will**
- Own the checkout and payments experience end to end
- Run A/B tests on conversion and payment-method mix
- Write user stories and manage the team's backlog and sprint priorities
- Work with our payment providers on integrations

**You bring**
- 4+ years as a product manager
- E-commerce or payments experience
- Comfort with SQL and experimentation tools
- Fluent English

This is an individual-contributor role with no direct reports.
