# Director of Product, Payroll Platform — Palkkasilta Oy

**Location:** Espoo, hybrid
**Salary:** €9,500–11,500 per month

Palkkasilta builds payroll and HR administration software for Finnish mid-sized employers. More than 2,400 companies run their monthly payroll, absences and incomes-register reporting on our platform.

We are looking for a Director of Product to lead four product teams and own the roadmap for payroll calculation, collective-agreement rules engines and integrations with the Incomes Register.

**Responsibilities**
- Own the payroll platform strategy and roadmap
- Lead four product managers
- Prioritise regulatory changes and keep the platform compliant every year
- Work closely with implementation consultants and customer success

**Requirements**
- 8+ years of product management, 3+ years leading product managers
- Experience with payroll, HR or other compliance-heavy B2B SaaS
- Fluent Finnish and English
