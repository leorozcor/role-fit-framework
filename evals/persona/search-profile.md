# Search profile — Alex Esimerkki (fictional eval persona)

*Invented for the evaluation cases. See `facts.md`.*

## Hard gates — any failure stops the process, no score needed

1. **Salary floor: €9,000/month base cash.** Not base-plus-illiquid-equity. Clearing only at the very top of a published band = conditional pass at best. A band that tops out below the floor is a fail.
2. **Geography: Helsinki-based.** Hybrid in Helsinki or remote within the Nordics and the EU. Up to about 30 travel days a year is fine when quantified; "regular travel" unquantified is a flag to resolve.
3. **Role is real, authorized, live, and genuinely open to an external hire.**
4. **No dealbreakers.** Gambling products.

## What a 9 looks like

- Owned leadership mandate over several product teams
- International operating reality: English as the working language, multi-country customers
- Fast, honest learning loops: consumer or marketplace products where value shows in data
- Kind and demanding culture
- Trust, safety or marketplace products

## What a 3 looks like

- "Influence without direct authority" as the core mechanism
- Accountability for commercial outcomes with advisory-only authority
- Domains with slow switching where product value can only be asserted

## Avoid list — established by repeated evidence

- **Payroll and HR administration software:** slow switching and long cycles; two earlier processes showed product value could not be demonstrated there.
- **Gambling.**

## Builder shape

- **Spikes (self-assessed):** trust and safety strategy, data fluency, developing product managers, cross-country alignment.
- **Weak zone (from selection evidence):** hands-on technical and AI-native execution; German-language markets.
- **Recent career identity:** product leadership at director level, several teams across countries.

## Standing principles

- A thin board is not evidence that rejected options have become better.
- Rapport is weak evidence of structural fit.
- A contact is not an advocate.
- Rejections update candidate-fit calibration only.
- **Framework freeze:** the eval persona never changes the framework.
