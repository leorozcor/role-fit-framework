### 1. Stop a verification launch that would have measured the wrong thing (Nordmarket, 2023)
- **Decision:** delay a mandatory ID-verification launch by six weeks and test an optional flow first.
- **Alternatives rejected:** launch mandatory verification in all three markets at once.
- **Reasoning and evidence:** usability tests showed sellers abandoning the mandatory flow; a launch would have measured friction, not trust.
- **What I did personally:** made the call with the engineering and design leads; presented it to the CPO.
- **Result:** the optional flow reached 40% verification in the first quarter (team result). No measured comparison with the mandatory alternative.
