# Network

Who can open a door, and how far. `/scan` and `/warm` read this file; only you edit it.
Copy it to `network.md` (gitignored) and fill it in.

**Strength** uses the framework's scale. Rate what the person has *done or said*, not how well you get along:
- **Contact** — knows you, might forward a CV. Does not count as a channel.
- **Advocate** — has told, or has agreed to tell, a hiring team why your background fits.
- **Sponsor** — has standing to materially influence a hiring decision.
- **unrated** — not yet assessed. Treated as Contact.

One line per person. `Company` is where they are now; add `knows:` in Notes for people who can reach into another company.
