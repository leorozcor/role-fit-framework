# Decision bank (template: copy to `decision-bank.md`, which stays private)

**Only the human edits this file.** Like `facts.md`, it is a source of claims: an entry here may be used in interviews and applications only as written. Estimates made after the fact are labelled *estimate* and never go on a CV.

*Why it exists (framework 3b):* hiring committees cannot observe judgment, so they look for substitutes: literal credentials, or **observable decisions**. Where a credential is missing (owned a revenue forecast, built a data product, shipped production AI), an evidenced decision is the only honest substitute. Aim for five or six. Lead with the decision and its result, not the context.

## Template

### <Short name> (<employer>, <year>)
- **Decision:** what was chosen, in one sentence.
- **Situation and constraint:** why a decision was needed; what made it hard.
- **Alternatives rejected:** the real options, and why not.
- **Reasoning and evidence:** what the choice rested on; what evidence would have changed it.
- **What I did personally:**
- **Result:** what happened, with numbers where they exist. Mark anything reconstructed as *estimate*.
- **Economic consequence:** cost, revenue, opportunity cost, risk taken or avoided.
- **What I would do differently:**
- **Proof threshold it serves:** e.g. investment case / forecasting judgment, trust outcomes, building a product organization, engineering collaboration.

## Entries

*Write five or six. Good candidates: a sequencing or investment decision, a launch you killed and rebuilt, a platform built once instead of several times, a trust or safety decision with a commercial cost, an organisational change with a measured effect.*
