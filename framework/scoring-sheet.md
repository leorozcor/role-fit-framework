## Verdict

☐ **Strong** — passes gates, beats the real alternative, no load-bearing warnings
☐ **Viable** — passes gates, does not clearly beat the real alternative
☐ **Watchlist** — insufficient evidence or not live enough
☐ **Pass** — fails gates, loses to real alternative, or double load-bearing warning
☐ **Gig** — bounded tour only: runway secured, other threads live, comp clears floor, exit expected
☐ **Real alternative / self-generated platform**

**Closing question — answer the one that applies:**

*Home:* Is this organization worthy of several years of my life and full emotional investment? ☐ Yes ☐ No
*Gig:* Is this worthy of a bounded period of excellent work, under explicit terms, with a clear reason for entering and leaving? ☐ Yes ☐ No

**Life compatibility:** What would this role demand from family, health, friendships, community or other meaningful pursuits — and is that exchange sustainable? ______
