## 3. Verify deployability

This verifies assumptions behind Mandate and Asymmetric Fit.
It does not create a new score.

- **Problem owner:** Who needs the outcome?
- **Sponsor:** Who will defend the mandate when priorities
  or incentives conflict?
- **Resources:** Which people, capabilities and budgets
  are actually committed?
- **Decision rights:** What can I decide alone? Who can
  override me, and under what circumstances?
- **Success:** What would demonstrate value after
  6–12 months? Who evaluates it?

## 4. Verify the actual contract

**Permanent employment**
- Employing entity, applicable terms, base and variable pay
- Probation, notice, location and travel expectations
- Side-work restrictions, non-competes and other obligations
- Material differences between the negotiated role
  and the written contract

**Consulting / bounded engagement**
- Framework agreement and assignment order; precedence
- Start, end, scope and expected versus guaranteed paid days
- Payment, expenses, notice and early termination
- Exclusivity, competing engagements and freedom to search
- Liability, insurance, IP and confidentiality
- Extension only through an agreed new commitment

## 5. Cost of saying yes

**Home:** Is this organization worthy of several years
of commitment and the effort required to succeed there?

**Gig:** Is this worth a bounded period of excellent work,
with a clear reason for entering and leaving?

**Opportunity cost:** What time, energy, earning capacity
or future options would accepting consume?

**Real alternative:** What happens if I decline or wait?
Use actual alternatives, not hypothetical offers.

**If two options are live:** compare them dimension by
dimension, not by score. Which one fails a minimum? Which
one's cost of saying yes is lower for the same career
capital?

**Regret test:** What is the strongest evidence-based
reason I might regret accepting?
